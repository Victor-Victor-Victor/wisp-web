    How to talk to WISP

    • Say the place first: "In the garage freezer, a chicken breast, and…"
    • End each item with "and" when more is coming. After a silence the list pauses (tap the mic to go on), and a while later it finishes by itself — both times are under Timing.
    • Say "that's it" when you're done. WISP reads the list back and asks about anything it wasn't sure of. Say "skip" to leave one item out, or "never mind" to skip the rest.
    • Undo removes the newest phrase of this list, one per tap. It's silent while WISP is listening, so the mic doesn't hear it.

    Android plays its own beep each time the mic opens. That means "listening", and WISP can't turn it off. Questions and the read-back are always spoken.
